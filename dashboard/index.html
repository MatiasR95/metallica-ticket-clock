How it is collected. A script running on GitHub's servers every 30 minutes, independent of any computer, reads TrackaLacker's public price pages for each show, plus the change logs of listings that moved. TicketSmarter's section-by-section list is the same public feed its event page loads. Nothing logs in, buys or gets past a captcha.

Middle sections. 104–108 and 204–208 face the middle of the stage. Metallica's own Diamond packages use 103–108 and 203–209 for the same block. TicketSmarter prices include its service fee, and TrackaLacker reports each site's price with fees where the site shows them, so compare a line with itself over time.
